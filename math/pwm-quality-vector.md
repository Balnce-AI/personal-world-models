# PWM Quality Vector

**Status:** `RESEARCH`

Candidate benchmark quality remains vector-valued:

$$
Q_{PWM}=(Cov,Fid,Temp,Cal,Prov,Conn,Pred,Act,Priv)
$$

The coordinates represent coverage, fidelity, temporal coherence, calibration, provenance quality, meaningful connectivity, predictive utility, action utility, and privacy/minimization. Every benchmark MUST define its own operationalization and opportunity set. Coordinates MUST NOT be averaged into a person-level intelligence, wellness, or consciousness score.
