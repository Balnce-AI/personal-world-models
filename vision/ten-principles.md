# Ten principles

1. **The person is the authority, not the profile.**
2. **World state is not the same thing as memory.**
3. **Observation, inference, belief, policy and intent remain distinguishable.**
4. **Provenance survives personalization.**
5. **Projection beats replication by default.**
6. **Authority precedes capability execution.**
7. **Foreign physical systems keep their safety veto.**
8. **Learning returns as evidence before it becomes self-knowledge.**
9. **Departure claims are measured, not magical.**
10. **Open interfaces should not require one vendor to own the person.**
