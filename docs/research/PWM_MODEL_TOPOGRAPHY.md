# PWM Model Topography

**Status:** `EXPERIMENTAL`

Topography is a vector diagnostic generated from model records and explicit edges. The reference reports model count, coverage heuristic, mean confidence, provenance quality, contradiction load, and connectivity in integer parts-per-million. It does not collapse these axes into a universal score and does not assess a person's worth, intelligence, or consciousness.

`DEPENDS_ON` edges must be acyclic; other semantic links may form graphs. Dangling edges and dependency cycles fail validation. Coverage is currently a fixture-oriented heuristic and must not be compared across datasets without a shared opportunity set.
