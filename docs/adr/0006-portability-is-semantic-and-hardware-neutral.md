# ADR-0006: Portability is semantic and hardware-neutral

- Status: `ACCEPTED`
- Scope: footprint profiles and exports

## Decision
Portability is preservation of public semantics, provenance and policy boundaries across micro, edge and full footprints, independent of hardware vendor or accelerator.

## Consequences
Hardware-affine dependencies are declared and replaceable. Successful serialization alone is not portability evidence.
