# Landscape map

**Status:** research synthesis; every public factual statement should be linked to a primary source before publication.

The relevant landscape is not one category.

| Actor / research family | Strongest adjacent idea | What it validates | Where PWM/HPL is structurally different |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stanford Human Context Protocol | portable, user-governed context/preferences across AI clients | personal context needs interoperability | PWM/HPL extends into provenance-rich world state, spatial/physical projection, authority and lifecycle |
| Personal AI | personal models/memory + distributed edge execution | personalized intelligence can be pushed toward network edge | canonical model sovereignty + multiple artifact types + physical/multi-principal projection rather than one model-centric strategy |
| Kinship / Personal World Models | explicit “Personal World Model” framing and portable personal representation | the market is independently arriving at world-model language | differentiate on formal semantics, provenance, authority, action, HPL, business/physical/network integration |
| Cereal / Cole | persistent embodied mind across robot bodies; capability contracts | intelligence and morphology can be decoupled | HPL centers the human/organization and foreign systems rather than one household/robot runtime |
| portable agent-memory research | portable memory serialization and provenance | memory needs lifecycle/lineage | PLOG/PWM is broader than memory and already has its own provenance substrate |
| Anthropic MHS | agent-facing hardware description/control abstraction | AI needs explicit machine capability/safety surfaces | HPL governs what human/organizational intelligence may be projected into those surfaces |
| ROS/COVESA/SOVD/WoT | mature domain runtimes, data models and device semantics | physical domains will stay protocol-diverse | HPL uses adapters/re-grounding rather than inventing one universal low-level bus/runtime |

## Claim discipline

Do not say “nobody else is doing this.”

The defensible claim is that no single system found in the research corpus clearly combines the entire architecture: sovereign user world model, provenance, temporal epistemics, selective projection, multi-principal authority, physical capability/safety separation, learning return, departure assurance, business fleets, edge placement, and an agent economy/network layer.

That is a **rare combination claim**, not proof of novelty of every component.
