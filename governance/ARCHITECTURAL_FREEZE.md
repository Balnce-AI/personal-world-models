# Temporary Architectural Freeze

**Effective:** 2026-10-05

**Boundary:** Public Personal World Model repository after Signed Semantic Conformance v1

The repository is at a temporary stabilization boundary. The current public provenance, signed semantic, model-ecology, HPL, SDK, adapter, and research surfaces should be tested, corrected, documented, and independently implemented before another major subsystem is added.

## Permitted Work

- bug and conformance corrections;
- security fixes and threat-model clarifications;
- documentation and runnable example improvements;
- support for independent external implementations;
- additional bounded conformance vectors;
- execution of existing benchmarks and preregistered research protocols;
- responses to external researcher and implementer questions.

## Held Work

Major ontology expansion, a new authority system, production federation, a new runtime substrate, production physical actuation, or absorption of NEP, FDX, Web0, Reasn, or other Balnce domains requires new evidence and explicit architectural authorization.

This freeze does not promote provisional or experimental interfaces. It protects the current evidence boundary while the architecture is tested from outside its originating implementation.
