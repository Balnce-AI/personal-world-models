# Code Of Conduct

This project welcomes technical disagreement, replication attempts, negative results, and evidence-based criticism. Participation must remain professional and safe.

## Expected Conduct

- Critique claims, methods, specifications, and code rather than people.
- Distinguish measured results from hypotheses and implementation from architecture.
- Disclose uncertainty, conflicts of interest, and material limitations.
- Use synthetic or properly authorized public data. Do not post personal world-model data, credentials, private correspondence, or identifying research records.
- Respect consent, privacy, attribution, intellectual property, and responsible disclosure boundaries.
- Avoid harassment, intimidation, discrimination, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and sustained disruption.

## Scope And Enforcement

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and project-organized spaces. Maintainers may edit, reject, hide, or remove contributions that violate it and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report security concerns through the process in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). For conduct concerns, email [zach@balnce.ai](mailto:zach@balnce.ai) with the subject `PWM conduct report` rather than publishing sensitive details. Maintainers will evaluate reports in context, protect confidentiality where practical, and avoid retaliation against good-faith reporters.

This policy is intentionally concise. It does not reduce anyone's legal rights or obligations.
