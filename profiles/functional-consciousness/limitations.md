# Limitations

- Functional access is not phenomenal consciousness.
- The catalog is a research prior, not an exhaustive ontology.
- Self-model count or topography is not a psychometric score of a person.
- No multiplicative cross-model aggregation is assumed.
- Catalog-level evaluation is blocked until all entries are verified against the versioned primary source.
- PWM benchmark outcomes can test utility of explicit structure; they cannot by themselves validate a consciousness theory.
