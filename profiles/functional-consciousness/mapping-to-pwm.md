# Mapping To PWM

The ten source domains are namespaced under `fc.*` and registered with `RESEARCH` status. They may classify a `ModelRecord`, but they do not define canonical PWM storage buckets.

An FC self-model may map to a PWM `SELF`, `OTHER`, `RELATIONSHIP`, or `META` model depending on subject and perspective. Evidence remains an assertion or provenance event; predictions and outcomes remain separate records; executable permission remains an authority decision, never a self-model belief.

PMRA can discover PWM-native families absent from the FC profile. Such extensions use `pwm.*` identifiers and document the divergence rather than being attributed to FC.
